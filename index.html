The other way to keep notes readable. DARKEN pushes the whole picture back; this carves a soft shadow that hugs the notes and leaves everything else alone, so a painting still looks like a painting. Scene Timeline [+]
A scene is the whole look — every colour, routing and slider — pinned to a timestamp. While the timeline runs, the playhead drives the panel instead of the other way round, so turn it off to go back to editing by hand. Scrubbing anywhere lands in the right look: nothing is remembered between frames, it is all worked out from the clock.
ADD A SCENE FROM A SAVED PRESET
Unlike the Presets dropdown further down, this does not change what is on screen — it drops the preset in as a scene at the playhead.
TIMENAMEFADE
→ parks the playhead on that scene. ↻ re-captures it from the panel as it looks right now — the way to edit a scene is to turn the timeline off, dial the look in, then hit it. × deletes. FADE is how many seconds the look takes to morph in; 0 is a hard cut. Volume, visual offset and frame size are deliberately left alone by scenes.
